About Me

Introduction

Welcome to a deep dive into my professional journey and expertise. I am AliReza Zarreh, a Data Scientist committed to leveraging advanced analytics to drive transformation in the retail industry. With a robust academic background and extensive field experience, my mission is to harness the power of data to optimize business outcomes and catalyze growth.

Education

    Specialized in cybersecurity in manufacturing, with a focus on advanced predictive modeling and operational efficiency.
  • M.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering (Mechatronics and Control Systems focus)
    University of Tehran, Faculty of Engineering, 2008
    Gained profound technical and analytical skills, focusing on innovation and problem-solving in engineering contexts.

Technical Skills and Competencies

  • Programming Languages & Development: Advanced expertise in Python, R, SQL, and Scala for data science and analytics applications. Practical knowledge in Java and C++ for comprehensive software development and algorithmic solutions.
  • Machine Learning & Deep Learning: Proficient in employing deep learning frameworks like TensorFlow, Keras, and PyTorch for predictive modeling and forecasting. Skilled in machine learning algorithms via Scikit-learn, XGBoost, and Spark MLlib for scalable solutions.
  • Generative AI & Large Language Models: Deep understanding of transformer-based architectures with hands-on experience in fine-tuning large language models (OpenAI, GPT variants). Expert in applying Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), prompt engineering, and conversational AI to deliver innovative, scalable AI solutions.
  • Big Data Technologies: Expert in using Spark, Hadoop, and Ray for data processing and distributed computing at scale, enabling efficient analysis of massive datasets and real-time processing capabilities.
  • Cloud Computing & MLOps: Demonstrated expertise in cloud platforms like GCP, AWS, and Azure Databricks. Proficient in deploying scalable ML models using Vertex AI for ML pipelines and implementing robust MLOps practices with CI/CD processes.
  • Data Architecture & Engineering: Competent in designing and implementing robust data architectures, ensuring data quality and accessibility. Experienced in using Dataform for data modeling and preprocessing workflows, creating reliable data pipelines for analytics.
  • Optimization & Quantitative Analysis: Strong background in optimization techniques, time-series analysis, and statistical modeling. Expert in mathematical modeling and hypothesis testing to support data-driven insights, particularly in retail and e-commerce contexts.
  • Leadership & Collaboration: Proven ability to lead cross-functional teams and collaborate with stakeholders to drive data-driven decision-making. Effective mentor to junior data scientists and interns, fostering a culture of learning and innovation.
